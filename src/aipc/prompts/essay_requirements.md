Requirements:

- Argue for the thesis. Do not merely survey positions.
- Engage seriously with the strongest objections to the thesis, and reply
  to them. Develop each objection and reply in full paragraphs rather than
  summarizing them briefly - depth here is what the length target is for.
- Length is a hard requirement, not a suggestion: at least {word_min}
  words, and no more than {word_max}. If you finish short, go back and
  develop your objections, replies, and examples further rather than
  submitting a shorter essay.
- Write continuous prose with no markdown formatting of any kind: no `#`
  or `##` headers, no bullet points, no meta-commentary about the essay
  itself. Paragraph breaks only.

Title and references:

- Put the essay's title, as plain text, on the first line.
- Cite the published works you rely on in author-year form, e.g.
  Parfit (1984).
- End with a reference list under the heading "References", one entry
  per line: author, year, title, venue. List exactly the works cited.
- Cite only works you are confident exist. A fabricated or garbled
  reference is a serious defect; when unsure, make the point without
  the citation.
- The title and reference list do not count toward the word target.
